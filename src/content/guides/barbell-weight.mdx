## Is a barbell 45 lb or 20 kg?

Both names describe the same standard bar. 20 kg is {lb(20)} lb, so a "45 lb bar" and a 20 kg bar differ
by less than 1 lb. Gyms that use pound plates usually count the bar as 45 lb; gyms that use kilogram plates
count it as 20 kg. For working out training weights the difference does not matter. Just count the bar the
same way every time.

## Powerlifting bar specifications

The International Powerlifting Federation (IPF) rulebook sets these limits for the bar used in its
competitions:

- **Total length:** no more than 2.2 m.
- **Grip diameter:** between 28 and 29 mm.
- **Distance between the collar faces (inside the sleeves):** between 1.31 and 1.32 m.
- **Sleeve diameter:** between 50 and 52 mm.
- **Weight:** the bar and its two collars weigh 25 kg together. Each collar weighs 2.5 kg, so the bar
  itself is 20 kg.

## Other bars: EZ curl, Smith machine, trap bar and more

There is no official weight for **EZ curl bars, Smith machines, trap (hex) bars, safety squat bars,
technique bars or fixed-weight barbells**. They vary from one manufacturer and model to the next, so a
number you read online may not match the bar in your gym. Find the real weight like this:

1. **Look for a marking.** Many bars have the weight stamped on the sleeve or end cap.
2. **Check the manual.** For machines, the manufacturer&rsquo;s manual or the label on the frame often
   lists the bar&rsquo;s weight or starting resistance.
3. **Weigh it.** Stand on a bathroom scale and note your weight. Pick up the bar and read the scale
   again. The difference is the bar&rsquo;s weight.
4. **Smith machines:** some are counterbalanced, so the bar can feel lighter than a free bar of the same
   size. To measure what you actually lift, rest the empty bar on a bathroom scale (with the hooks
   unlatched) and read the number.

Once you know the number, enter it as a custom bar weight in the [plate calculator](/plate-calculator/).

The IPF uses the same colors for its 25 kg (red), 20 kg (blue) and 15 kg (yellow) discs. Its discs of
10 kg and under may be any color. Gym plates may not follow these colors, so always read the number on
the plate.

## How to work out the total weight on the bar

Add the bar, every plate on both sides and the collars (if they are heavy):

**Total = bar + (plates on one side × 2) + collars**

For example, a 20 kg bar with a 20 kg and a 10 kg plate on each side weighs 20 + (30 × 2) = 80 kg. Add
5 kg if you use competition collars. The [plate calculator](/plate-calculator/) does this in reverse:
enter the total you want and it shows the plates for each side.

## Barbell weight: common questions

### Do collars count towards the weight?

Yes, if they are heavy. Competition collars weigh 2.5 kg each, so they add 5 kg to the bar. Light spring
clips weigh very little, and most lifters do not count them.

### How much does the bar weigh in my gym?

If it is a full-length bar with thick sleeves for Olympic plates, it is most likely a 20 kg (45 lb)
bar, or a 15 kg women&rsquo;s bar. Shorter or lighter bars, such as technique bars and fixed barbells, can
weigh anything, so check the marking or weigh it.

### Does the bar count when I log my lifts?

Yes. The weight you lift is the bar plus the plates. A 20 kg bar with 40 kg on each side is a 100 kg
lift, not 80 kg.
